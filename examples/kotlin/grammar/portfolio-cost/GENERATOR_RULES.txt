`cott_runtime.CottList` implements Kotlin's read-only `List`; iterate `rows` directly and never assume a mutable backing collection. Preserve list order and the declared first-error priority. Use `Long`-to-`Double` conversion, then a separate binary64 multiplication and addition for each holding; do not use `Math.fma`, `BigDecimal`, reordering, or aggregation helpers. Construct the declared `cott_runtime.Ok` and `cott_runtime.Err` results with generated `PortfolioError` variants.
