Kotlin has no fixture I/O adapters: perform file, HTTP and SQLite effects directly with JDK APIs (java.nio.file, java.net.http, java.sql) on the supplied Path or URL. Scenario files.path(...) and service.url(...) arguments arrive already resolved; CottRuntime.fixturePath/fixtureUrl and their suspend forms are runner lookup APIs, not file, network, database or clock hooks.
